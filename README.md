## Using the logo

- Write the name as one word with a capital R: Rulebook.
- Use the full logo when people may not know the name yet, and the mark on its own where they already do.
- Use the blue or black logo on light backgrounds, and the white one on blue or dark backgrounds.
- Use the files as they are: don't stretch, crop, recolor, or add shadows or effects.
- Leave space around the logo, and don't put it on a busy photo.
